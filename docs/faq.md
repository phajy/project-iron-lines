# Answers to some FAQs

- $a$ is the dimensinless spin parameter with $-1 \le a \le 1$ (-ve implies the BH is rotating in the opposide sense to the disc).
- The Kerr solution is for a general rotating black hole with spin parameter $a$, which reduces to the staitc Schwarzschild solution when $a = 0$.
- There is an Innermost Stable Cicular Orbit (ISCO), the radius of which depends on the black hole spin, $a$. The ISCO decreases from $r = 6 GM/c^2 = 6 r_g$ to $r \approx 1.23$ for $a = 0.998$ (the astrophysical limit for $a$ because photons captured from the accretion disc remove angular momentum from the black hole).
- How can we measure black hole spin? Reflection spectroscopy $\to$ gravitational redshifting and Doppler shifts $\to$ larger redshifts at smaller radii $\to$ more broadening $\to$ measure ISCO $\to$ infrer $a$ from the data.
